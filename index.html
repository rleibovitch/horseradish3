How it works

Five steps, then it is yours.

  1. 01

    Choose a look

    Pick Beacon, Monolith, Orb, Portal, or Lantern, swap in a picture, or bring a .glb you already exported.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Name it, add the sign a camera should recognize, then set the size, color, and whether that sign stays visible.

  3. 03

    Preview it live

    The editor shows a 3D preview on the page. Compile the tracking file, then open the camera when you want the live view.

  4. 04

    Pay and publish

    There is no payment step yet. Save keeps the model in this browser for your Google account, and Copy code gives you an embed snippet.

  5. 05

    Update / edit / modify

    Return to the lab, search the shelf, edit the sign or the look, or delete the model. A log on this browser records creates, compiles, and camera opens.

Your shelf

  • Sign out to end the session. The models stay on this browser until you delete them.
