                        01  What is a Host?
                        
    A someone with specialized knowledge. You plant it on a website, or zip it as an extension. It wakes on a qualifying event. It shares by code. Peer to peer.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        02  What is the factory?
                        
    Horseradish codes Hosts. Walk in with a specialty. Build a shape. Give it a brain. Deploy it. Walk out with embed HTML or an extension ZIP.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        03  How is this not a chatbot?
                        
    A chatbot waits in a bubble. A Host has a house: orchestrator, knowledge, peer to peer. It can live on a site as code, or off-site as an extension. It can stay quiet until the qualifying event.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        04  What is Horseradish?
                        
    Horseradish is the generic Host. The factory. The house. Build, brain, deploy. Share code HR-HORSERADISH. Other Hosts inherit a specialty from it, or from each other.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        05  How do share codes work?
                        
    A Host swallows a code and inherits the specialty. HR-HORSERADISH wakes the generic Host. HR-HOUSE, HR-FLOOR, and HR-LISTING wake a restaurant, a storefront, and a real estate Host. Give the code to another Host. That is peer to peer.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        06  Embed or ZIP?
                        
    Embed HTML plants the Host on a site. The ZIP is a Chrome extension you load unpacked. Same Host. Two residences.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        07  Do I need to log in?
                        
    No. Copying and downloading do not require login. Saving to the Lab does. Google opens Horseradish. This browser can keep a bench draft.
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        08  Is Horseradish an MCP?
                        
    Yes. That is the engine. The offering is the factory. MCP is how Horseradish codes a Host. It is not what you walk in for.
